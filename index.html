What if we miss a payment?

Nothing disappears overnight. We keep your world as long as you're paying, and we give you plenty of time and warnings if a payment is missed.

  1. First 30 daysGrace periodEverything keeps working while we remind you.
  2. Days 31 to 90SleepingYou can still view and download everything. New posts pause.
  3. Day 91Safely storedThe world goes offline and we email you a full copy.
  4. Up to 3 yearsWake it any timeOne year of hosting plus $29 brings it all back. We warn you 60, 30 and 7 days before anything is deleted.
